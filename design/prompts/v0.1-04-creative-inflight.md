# Prompt 04 — Creative: In-Flight Hero Shot

**Design version:** v0.1 · rev. 2 (2026-08-05) · **Purpose:** cinematic action image
**Suggested size:** 16:9 (state it in the prompt)
**Source:** [`../Salamandra-Design-Guide-v0.1.md`](../Salamandra-Design-Guide-v0.1.md)
**Technique notes:** cinematic lighting + telephoto compression + motion cues (prop
blur); a banked attitude makes the forward-swept planform readable; aspect ratio and
camera explicitly defined; see [`README.md`](README.md).

## Prompt

> **Role.** Act as an aviation photographer shooting a drone in flight.
>
> **Subject.** Salamandra: a 1300 mm wingspan 3D-printed forward-swept tailless flying
> wing. Light gray PETG with visible FDM layer lines catching the low sun. Strongly
> forward-swept tapered wing (quarter-chord sweep −20°, root chord 289 mm, tip chord
> 145 mm), slight 2° dihedral at the tips, three printed segments per wing half with
> visible joint lines. Central pod (CORE) with rounded nose, single pusher propeller at
> the rear spinning as a semi-transparent motion blur, large elevons along the trailing
> edge slightly deflected, small FPV camera in the nose glinting. No tail, no vertical
> stabilizer.
>
> **Medium.** Photorealistic aviation photograph.
>
> **Environment.** Mountain valley at golden hour; low warm sun, long shadows, slight
> haze in the distance.
>
> **Composition.** Telephoto compression look (200 mm lens); the wing is banked ~30°
> toward the camera, revealing the forward-swept planform clearly; propeller visible as
> a spinning blur, aircraft tack sharp.
>
> **Lighting.** Low warm sun from behind the aircraft, rim light on the leading edges.
>
> **Color.** Slightly desaturated warm color grade.
>
> **Mood.** Dramatic, professional, epic but credible.
>
> **Format.** 16:9 widescreen.
>
> **Exclusions.** No text, no logos, no watermark, no pilots, no airbrushed sci-fi
> elements.
>
> **Quality.** Highly detailed, sharp focus on the airframe, realistic motion blur only
> on the propeller disc, subtle atmospheric haze.

## Variants (same chat, multi-turn)

- **Variant B — FPV perspective:** "First-person view from the onboard camera: the nose
  and the forward-swept leading edges visible at the bottom corners of the frame,
  valley ahead, propeller not visible (pusher)."
- **Variant C — Night:** "Lit by small red and green navigation LEDs, dark blue dusk
  sky, propeller blur, city lights below."
- **Variant D — Head-on climb:** "Straight-on view from below as it climbs steeply,
  showing the full forward-swept planform against the sky."

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Forward-swept planform clearly readable in the banked attitude
- [ ] No tail, no vertical stabilizer
- [ ] Pusher propeller with motion blur (not a tractor prop)
- [ ] 3D-printed texture consistent with the v0.1 design
- [ ] 16:9 ratio respected
