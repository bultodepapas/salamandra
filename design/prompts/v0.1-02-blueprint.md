# Prompt 02 — Technical Blueprint

**Design version:** v0.1 · rev. 2 (2026-08-05) · **Purpose:** engineering drawing (three-view plan)
**Suggested size:** 4:3 or 1:1 (state it in the prompt)
**Source:** [`../Salamandra-Design-Guide-v0.1.md`](../Salamandra-Design-Guide-v0.1.md)
**Technique notes:** minimal text (models still fail at rendering text — OpenAI
documented limitation); simple, structured layout (composition control is limited);
see [`README.md`](README.md).

## Prompt

> **Role.** Act as a technical draughtsman producing an engineering drawing sheet.
>
> **Subject.** Salamandra: a 1300 mm wingspan 3D-printed forward-swept tailless flying
> wing. Geometry: strongly forward-swept tapered wing (quarter-chord sweep −20°, root
> chord 289 mm, tip chord 145 mm), slight 2° dihedral at the tips, three printed
> segments per wing half with joint lines, central fuselage pod (CORE) with rounded nose
> and a single pusher propeller at the rear, large elevons along the trailing edge with
> hinge lines at 72 % of the chord, small FPV camera in the nose. No tail, no vertical
> stabilizer.
>
> **Medium.** Classic technical blueprint drawing, orthographic three-view: top view,
> front view and side view, precise drafting style with thin construction lines and
> dimension lines.
>
> **Environment.** Plain blueprint sheet, deep blue background, white lines.
>
> **Composition.** Three views laid out on a single sheet: top view centered, front view
> below it, side view to the right; title block in the bottom-right corner.
>
> **Lighting.** Not applicable — flat technical line drawing, no shading.
>
> **Color.** White lines on deep blue background only.
>
> **Mood.** Precise, minimal, technical.
>
> **Format.** 1:1 square sheet.
>
> **Exclusions.** No other colors, no logos, no watermark, no extra annotations.
>
> **Quality.** Highly detailed, clean straight lines, sharp focus.
>
> **Text (keep minimal — models fail at long text).** Only the title block reads
> "SALAMANDRA V0.1" and the wingspan note "1300 MM". Include only four dimension
> labels: 1300, 289, 145, −20°.

## Iteration workflow (same chat, multi-turn)

1. Generate. Check the checklist — sweep direction is the #1 failure.
2. "White background, black lines, engineering drawing style like a CAD drawing sheet"
   (CAD variant).
3. "Add dashed lines showing the three printed segments per wing half and the central
   pod joint."
4. If the text is garbled: "Ignore all other text; only the title block 'SALAMANDRA
   V0.1' and the labels 1300 / 289 / 145 / −20° are required."
5. If the sweep is wrong: state it explicitly — "the wing tips must point FORWARD of
   the root."

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Top view shows a forward-swept planform (tips ahead of root)
- [ ] Front view shows the 2° dihedral and no vertical stabilizer
- [ ] Side view shows the pusher propeller at the rear
- [ ] Dimensions read 1300 / 289 / 145 mm and sweep −20°
- [ ] Title block text is legible (accept a small error; regenerate rather than
      over-editing)
