# Prompt 03 — Realistic Photograph

**Design version:** v0.1 · rev. 2 (2026-08-05) · **Purpose:** photorealistic image of the physical model
**Suggested size:** 3:2 (state it in the prompt)
**Source:** [`../Salamandra-Design-Guide-v0.1.md`](../Salamandra-Design-Guide-v0.1.md)
**Technique notes:** explicit camera/lens/lighting (style modifiers and shot types);
photorealism needs imperfection cues (print seams, dust, shallow DOF); scale anchor;
see [`README.md`](README.md).

## Prompt

> **Role.** Act as a photographer documenting a newly 3D-printed model in a workshop.
>
> **Subject.** Salamandra: a 1300 mm wingspan 3D-printed forward-swept tailless flying
> wing. Light gray PETG plastic with clearly visible FDM layer lines, matte finish,
> slight print artifacts at the seams between the three printed segments per wing half.
> Forward-swept tapered wing (quarter-chord sweep −15°, root chord 289 mm, tip
> chord 145 mm), slight 2° dihedral at the tips. Central fuselage pod (CORE) with
> rounded nose, battery hatch and a black 8-inch (203 mm) two-blade pusher propeller
> mounted at the rear. Large elevons along the trailing edge with hinge lines at 72 % of
> the chord. No tail, no vertical stabilizer. Small FPV camera lens in the nose.
>
> **Medium.** Photorealistic photograph.
>
> **Environment.** Resting on a wooden workbench in a small workshop; a measuring tape
> and a couple of tools on the bench give a sense of scale.
>
> **Composition.** Three-quarter view, camera at table height, subject slightly angled;
> shallow depth of field with the background softly blurred.
>
> **Lighting.** Natural window light from the left, soft and directional.
>
> **Color.** Realistic muted palette, natural plastic tones.
>
> **Mood.** Honest, documentary, maker-workshop atmosphere.
>
> **Format.** 3:2 landscape.
>
> **Exclusions.** No text, no logos, no watermark, no people, no dramatic filters.
>
> **Quality.** Highly detailed, realistic plastic texture, sharp focus on the wing,
> 50 mm lens look, subtle film grain.

## Iteration workflow (same chat, multi-turn)

1. Generate. Check the checklist — sweep direction and propeller side are the top
   failures.
2. "A person's hand holding the wing by the central pod" (scale variant — hands are a
   model weakness; keep the grip simple and re-check).
3. "Same setup but on short grass at a flying field, overcast light" (outdoor variant).
4. "Show it from the rear, propeller and elevon hinge visible."
5. If the sweep is wrong: state it explicitly — "the wing tips must point FORWARD of
   the root."

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Forward-swept planform (tips ahead of the root)
- [ ] Printed PETG look: layer lines, matte, seam artifacts at segment joints
- [ ] Pusher propeller at the rear center (not a tractor prop at the nose)
- [ ] No tail, no vertical stabilizer
- [ ] Scale cues present (tape or hand)
